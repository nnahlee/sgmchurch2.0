IRS REQUIREMENTS
A $50 donation → generate a regular donation receipt.
A $300 donation → generate a receipt that also serves as the special written acknowledgment.


1. Donor makes a donation
The donor pays through Stripe or PayPal.

2. Your backend confirms the payment
A verified webhook from the payment provider confirms the donation succeeded. This prevents someone from generating a document just by visiting a URL

3. Generate a PDF acknowledgment
Include the nonprofit's legal name, donation date and amount, donor information if available, and the required tax acknowledgment language.

4. Email the document
Send the PDF to the donor using your existing email setup, with an option to download it from the confirmation page.
